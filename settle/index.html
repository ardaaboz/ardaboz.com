settle

A quiet puzzle about finding your place.

Swipe to tilt the world. Everything loose slides at once, the pebble and the stones alike, until something stops it. Help the pebble roll home into its hollow.

Android · coming to Google Play

The first four levels of the game, with its own rules. Swipe the board, or click it and use the arrow keys.

One rule

Many small worlds

Still water swallows the pebble, but a stone rolled into it becomes a path. Moss holds whatever rolls onto it. Rails carry you round the bend, thin ice holds only once, and a glowing ember burns the moss away. Then wells, currents, bells and glass, a hollow that wanders, two pebbles that move as one, and one that mirrors you.

In the game

Made slowly, by hand

Levels
212 in 18 chapters, and 23 harder ones in the bonus chapter, Deep.
Every one solvable
Each level is checked by a solver before it ships, so there is always a way home, and a fewest-moves count to aim for if you like.
Calm
No timers, no lives, no ads. Undo is always free.
Sound
Soft music composed as you play, in the key of each chapter. Every stop is a note in that key.
Pebbles
Forty to collect, one at the end of every chapter. Some leave a little weather behind them as they roll.
For every eye
Night and day themes, a high-contrast mode with a shape on every piece, and reduced motion.
Languages
English, Türkçe, 한국어, 日本語.
Price
One price for the whole game. Three small extras if you want them: more hints, every chapter opened at once, or a pebble you like.

Privacy

Settle collects nothing

No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Your progress, settings and pebbles stay on your phone, and nothing is sent to anyone.

Purchases are handled entirely by the app store; we never see payment details.
